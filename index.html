My
Boundaries

A trial, not a commitment.

I

Going back into the group

  • Return as a trial, not a commitment.
  • Visit between 2pm and 6pm UK time.
  • Judge whether the group has moved on by who turns up and what the chat is like.
  • Judge over about four visits across two weeks, not one evening.
  • If it hasn't moved on, drift away quietly, with no announcement.
II

On Instagram

  • Five comments per session at most.
  • Comment properly, not with quick generic replies.
  • Only comment on posts from people I have a genuine relationship with, not whatever comes first in the feed.
  • Memorial posts only for people I know well.
  • No obligation to post birthday wishes.
  • Keep trimming the follow list to the people I actually interact with.

Fewer interactions is useful filtering, not a loss.

III

Reaching out

  • DM a few people I miss, particularly those who have left the group or rarely appear.

A slow reply, or no reply, is not a verdict. They may be as wary as I am.
